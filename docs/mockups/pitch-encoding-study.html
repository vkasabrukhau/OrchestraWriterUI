Two ways to put pitch on the stage

The same excerpt plays on both stages. On the left, the core's size shows the written dynamic and its brightness shows pitch. On the right, size shows the dynamic and the core's position in the seat shows pitch. Neither reads the audio.
The excerpt is 16 bars written for this mockup (D minor, 96 bpm), synthesized in the browser. All pitches are sounding pitches.

What to look for

  1. Bars 1–4. Only cellos, basses, timpani and bassoon, pp to mp, low in their ranges. With the lower ceilings in A, is the opening still readable?
  2. Bar 13. fff tutti. Every core in A fills its seat. Do rows stay separate, or do the halos join up?
  3. Bar 16. Violin I holds a high D at pp. In A that is a small, bright core. Does it read as quiet?
  4. Size against brightness. Try "Everyone pp, high" and "Everyone fff, low". Can you tell which stage is louder without reading the labels?
  5. Number of hues. The register palette in A uses 14 hues. The project rule caps it at 8–10. Switch A to "Hue by section" to compare.
